Raspberry Pi 4/5 (~$60-90)

  - Runs Linux + Docker natively
  - Pi 5 with 4GB RAM handles 4 small containers comfortably
  - Tiny, low power (~5W), passively cooled
  - Risk: SD card reliability for a database (use an SSD over USB instead)

  Mini PC / Intel N100 (~$100-200)

  - Full x86, better Docker compatibility than ARM
  - Examples: Beelink S12 Pro, GMKtec M5
  - More headroom, built-in eMMC or M.2 SSD
  - Still very small and low power

  Industrial SBC / Embedded PC (~$150-400)

  - Designed for 24/7 field deployment, wide temp range, no moving parts
  - Examples: Advantech, Onlogic, Seeed reComputer
  - Worth considering if the machines are in harsh environments (utility rooms, outdoors)
